Kura Ya Maisha Yako
The public face

Report, check, learn, before the lie spreads

Kura Ya Maisha Yako is the citizen app. It works offline, installs from a link, and keeps reporters anonymous by default.

  1. Submit a report: vote buying, intimidation, a polling-station problem or a rumour.
  2. Switch to the dashboard and watch it arrive in the verification queue.
  3. Verify it there, and it goes live on the county map.
